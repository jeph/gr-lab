The map in GR Photo

The map of where a photo was taken is drawn by MapLibre Native, whose license
is in this list under maplibre_gl.

Its tiles and the fonts of its labels are not part of GR Photo: they are
fetched from OpenFreeMap (https://openfreemap.org), which serves
OpenStreetMap data in the OpenMapTiles schema.

Map data: (c) OpenStreetMap contributors, under the Open Database License,
https://www.openstreetmap.org/copyright
Schema: (c) OpenMapTiles, https://www.openmaptiles.org/
Fonts: Noto Sans, under the SIL Open Font License 1.1.

The map's style (assets/map/style.json) is OpenFreeMap's Positron as served
on 2026-11-12 (https://github.com/hyperknot/openfreemap-styles, commit
006ea57, styles/positron), a fork of OpenMapTiles' Positron GL Style
(https://github.com/openmaptiles/positron-gl-style). GR Photo draws it in the
colours of its own theme and leaves out the shaded relief, the road shields
and the icons. Both are used under the licenses below, not under GR Photo's
MIT License.
