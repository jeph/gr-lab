Photos in GR Lab

The photos and video of the simulated camera in demo mode (assets/demo/) are
from Unsplash (https://unsplash.com), fetched through Lorem Picsum
(https://picsum.photos). They are used under the Unsplash License below, not
under GR Lab's MIT License. Each was resized, some were cropped, the tall ones
are stored on their side as a camera stores them, and one was made into a short
video.

The photos of the welcome screen, which are also the look editor's sample
photos, are GR Lab's own. Jeph took them with a GR IV and a GR IV HDF, and they
are under GR Lab's MIT License like the rest of the app:
assets/samples/sample01, assets/samples/sample02, assets/samples/sample03,
assets/samples/sample04, assets/samples/sample05 and assets/samples/sample06.
